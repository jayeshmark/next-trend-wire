Players whose careers began before 1999 show only their totals from 1999 onward.

Team labels use the abbreviation in use that season: STL for the Rams through 2015, SD for the Chargers through 2016, and OAK for the Raiders through 2019.

This is the expanded edition: top 50 plus ties. The standard edition, top 25 plus ties, is on the passing records page.

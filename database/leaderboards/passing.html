Jordan Love leads 2026 with 2,838 passing yards through Week 10; Tom Brady leads career totals with 102,616 yards, 1999 to 2026.

2026 totals are partial, through Week 10. Twelve of the fourteen Week 10 games are in the data. The Chargers at Ravens game and the Steelers at Bengals game had no player stats in this pull, so the Ravens, Bengals, Chargers, and Steelers are each missing one game. Career totals combine regular season and postseason, 1999 to 2026, including the partial 2026 season. Players whose careers began before 1999 are shown with only their 1999 onward totals, because earlier seasons are not in this data.

Passer rating uses the standard NFL formula, from completions, attempts, yards, touchdowns, and interceptions, on a scale from 0 to 158.3. Yards, touchdowns, and interceptions in these tables are passing totals.

NFL snap counts leaders

Darnell Wright and Joe Thuney share the 2026 offense lead with 660 snaps through Week 10; Alex Singleton leads 2026 defense with 647 snaps.

2026 totals are partial, through Week 10. Twelve of the fourteen Week 10 games are in the data. The Chargers at Ravens game and the Steelers at Bengals game had no player stats in this pull, so the Ravens, Bengals, Chargers, and Steelers are each missing one game. The Bears, Broncos, Eagles, and Buccaneers had a bye in Week 10. Data pulled on November 16, 2026.

Snap counts cover 2013 to 2026. Snap share is the player's snaps divided by the total snaps played by the player's team in the games in the data, worked out from per game snap percentages. Offense and defense snaps are counted separately, so a player can appear in both tables. No snap share is shown for career totals, because teams and roles change across seasons.

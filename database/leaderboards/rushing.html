NFL rushing leaders

Jahmyr Gibbs leads 2026 with 1,000 rushing yards through Week 10; Frank Gore leads career totals with 16,668 yards, 1999 to 2026.

2026 totals are partial, through Week 10. Twelve of the fourteen Week 10 games are in the data. The Chargers at Ravens game and the Steelers at Bengals game had no player stats in this pull, so the Ravens, Bengals, Chargers, and Steelers are each missing one game. The Bears, Broncos, Eagles, and Buccaneers had a bye in Week 10. Data pulled on November 16, 2026. Career totals combine regular season and postseason, 1999 to 2026, including the partial 2026 season. Players whose careers began before 1999 are shown with only their 1999 onward totals, because earlier seasons are not in this data.

Yards per attempt is rushing yards divided by attempts.

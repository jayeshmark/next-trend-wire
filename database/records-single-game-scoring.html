Single game scoring records

The best single game scoring performances from 1999 to 2026, with 2026 partial through Week 10. Total touchdowns exclude passing touchdowns.

Coverage: 1999 to 2026. Regular season and postseason are combined. The 2026 season is partial, through Week 10. Only 12 of 14 Week 10 games had landed in the source data at build time, so 2026 totals and ranks will change as the season continues. Players whose careers began before 1999 show only their totals from 1999 onward.

Team labels use the abbreviation in use that season: STL for the Rams through 2015, SD for the Chargers through 2016, and OAK for the Raiders through 2019.

← Back to the NFL stats section

Total touchdowns formula: rushing touchdowns + receiving touchdowns + special teams touchdowns + defensive touchdowns + fumble recovery touchdowns. Passing touchdowns are not counted. Points formula: total touchdowns x 6 + field goals made x 3 + PAT made x 1. Special teams touchdowns include kickoff return and punt return touchdowns.

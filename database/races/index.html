How the projections work

Formula. Projected final total equals current total divided by games played, times 17.

Games played counts the games with a weekly data row for that player. A bye week or a missed game does not count, so a player who missed time is judged on the games actually played, not on the league week number. Team games remaining is 17 minus the games the player's team has already played, from the schedules file. The regular season is 17 games per team. Postseason games are not included anywhere in this section.

Players tied on current total share a rank, and every player tied at the fifth place total is shown, so some race tables hold six rows. Each race page also has a what if calculator: choose a player, enter a rate per game from here, and it adds that rate times the player's remaining team games to the current total. The calculator is arithmetic, not a forecast.

A projection is arithmetic from past games. It is not a prediction, a pick, or odds, and it does not account for injuries, matchups, or playing time changes.

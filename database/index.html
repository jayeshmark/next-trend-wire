NFL stats

This section holds NFL numbers from the 1999 season through the 2025 season, in plain tables with no paywall, no ads, and no login needed.

It covers three things. First, team seasons: one page per team, with that team's season by season record. Second, player careers: one page per player, with that player's totals across the seasons in this data. Third, records: the top 25 career and single season totals for passing, rushing, receiving, scoring, and defense.

All totals here combine the regular season and the postseason. Only totals are shown, so there are no rate stats and no minimum thresholds to argue about. A player's career total is the sum of that player's season totals in this data, 1999 to 2025.

Records

  • Passing records Career and single season passing yards and passing touchdowns.
  • Rushing records Career and single season rushing yards and rushing touchdowns.
  • Receiving records Career and single season receptions, receiving yards, and receiving touchdowns.
  • Scoring records Career and single season total touchdowns and points.
  • Defensive records Career and single season sacks, interceptions, and combined tackles.

Teams

Team pages are grouped by the abbreviation used in the data. The 32 abbreviations in this data are:

Historical teams: STL (St. Louis Rams) SD (San Diego Chargers) OAK (Oakland Raiders)

Players by last name

Every player in this data is listed under the first letter of the player's surname. Suffixes like Jr. and III are ignored, so Kenneth Walker III is listed under W. Pick a letter to see every player with that initial.

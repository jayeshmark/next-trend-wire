What each trait means

Each trait is one career total from the player chosen for it: passing yards and passing touchdowns, rushing yards and rushing touchdowns, receptions, receiving yards and receiving touchdowns, tackles, sacks, defensive interceptions, field goals made, punting yards, and return yards. Returns combine punt returns and kickoff returns. Tackles combine solo tackles and assisted tackles, and the table shows the split.

The career leaders button fills every trait with the player who holds the highest career total in that trait. When two players tie, the first in alphabetical order is used.

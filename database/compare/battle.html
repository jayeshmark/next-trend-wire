How the battle is scored

A category counts toward the overall score only when both players have stats in that category. Inside a counted category, each stat row goes to the higher total, except interceptions thrown, which goes to the lower total, and only when both players attempted a pass. A tied row goes to neither player. The category winner is the player who wins the most rows in that category. A category where only one player has stats is shown in the table but does not count toward the overall score, and a category where neither player has stats is listed below the table.

The battle scores yards and touchdowns, receptions, tackles, sacks, interceptions, field goals, punts, and returns. Attempts and targets are not scored here. For the full side by side table with attempts and targets, use the compare tool.

Returns combine punt returns and kickoff returns. Tackles combine solo tackles and assisted tackles. This is a stats battle over career totals. It is not a prediction about any game.

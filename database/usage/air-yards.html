NFL air yards leaders

Matthew Golden leads the NFL with 1,375 air yards through Week 10 of the 2026 season. The tables below rank air yards and air yards share for 2026 and for the complete 2025 season.

2026 figures are regular season only, through Week 10. Week 10 is partial: 12 of 14 scheduled games are in the data used for this update, so totals will rise when the remaining games are added. 2025 figures are the complete regular season, Weeks 1 to 18.

Air yards count how far downfield the ball traveled in the air on each target, measured from the line of scrimmage to the catch point or the spot where the pass fell incomplete. They measure the depth of the throw, not yards gained after the catch. Air yards share is a player's air yards divided by the team's total air yards over the same period. A player who changed teams is listed under the team of the most recent game in the data, and the share uses that team's total.

Tables are limited to wide receivers, tight ends, and running backs, the positions that draw targets.

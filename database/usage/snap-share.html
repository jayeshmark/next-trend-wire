NFL snap share leaders

Darnell Wright has played the most offensive snaps in 2026, 660 through Week 9. The tables below rank offensive snaps, defensive snaps, and skill position snaps for 2026 and for the complete 2025 season.

2026 snap figures are regular season only, through Week 9. Week 10 snap counts covered only 1 of 14 scheduled games at the time of this update, so Week 10 is left out of the 2026 snap tables rather than shown partial. 2025 figures are the complete regular season, Weeks 1 to 18.

Snap share is a player's snaps divided by the team's total snaps on the same side of the ball, counting only games in which the player appeared. Team totals are derived from the per game shares in the snap count release. Snap counts in this data start with the 2013 season.

The skill position tables are limited to running backs, wide receivers, and tight ends.

NFL red zone stats

Jahmyr Gibbs leads the NFL with 56 red zone touches through Week 10 of the 2026 season. The tables below rank red zone touches, carries, and targets for 2026 and for the complete 2025 season.

2026 figures are regular season only, through Week 10. Week 10 is partial: 12 of 14 scheduled games are in the data used for this update, so totals will rise when the remaining games are added. 2025 figures are the complete regular season, Weeks 1 to 18.

The red zone is the area inside the opponent's 20 yard line. A red zone touch is a rushing attempt or a pass target on a play that started inside the opponent's 20. Two point tries are not counted. A passing touchdown is credited to the receiver and a rushing touchdown to the runner. Games counts games in which the player had at least one red zone touch.

NFL targets leaders

CeeDee Lamb leads the NFL with 121 targets through Week 10 of the 2026 season. The tables below rank targets and target share for 2026 and for the complete 2025 season.

2026 figures are regular season only, through Week 10. Week 10 is partial: 12 of 14 scheduled games are in the data used for this update, so totals will rise when the remaining games are added. 2025 figures are the complete regular season, Weeks 1 to 18.

Target share is a player’s targets divided by the team’s total targets over the same period. It shows how much of a team's passing volume went to one player. A player who changed teams is listed under the team of the most recent game in the data, and the share uses that team's total.

Tables are limited to wide receivers, tight ends, and running backs, the positions that draw targets.

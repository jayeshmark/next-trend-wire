NFL usage stats

Targets, air yards, snaps, and red zone touches for the 2026 and 2025 seasons.

These pages refresh on the Tuesday and Friday update cycle during the season. When a week is only partly in the data, the affected tables say so on the page and totals update as the remaining games arrive. All figures are regular season only.

← Back to the NFL stats section

Usage stats measure involvement rather than production: who gets targeted, who stays on the field, and who gets the ball near the goal line. A target is a pass thrown to a player. Air yards measure the depth of those throws. Snap share shows how much a player was on the field. Red zone touches count carries and targets inside the opponent's 20 yard line. Together they show opportunity, which changes week to week during the season.

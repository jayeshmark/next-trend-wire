How to check this page

Every number on this page comes from ratings-data.json, which holds the published inputs, the pool averages and spreads, and the published weights for every qualifying player. The ranking above is computed in your browser from that file. The full methodology, including thresholds, limitations, and what the rating does not measure, is published on each position page: quarterback, running back, wide receiver, tight end.

Source: nflverse play by play data, 1999 to 2026, regular season and postseason combined. Figures are presented in this site's own layout. See the data sources page for credits.

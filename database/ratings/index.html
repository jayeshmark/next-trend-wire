Open formula player ratings

Player ratings with the formula printed on the page. Every input is shown, every weight is stated, and the pool averages used to score each input are published, so any reader with the same play by play data gets the same numbers.

2026 ratings cover Weeks 1 to 10. Week 10 is partial: 13 of 14 games are scored. All scored games have play by play data. Not in this data: LAC at BAL (2026-11-16). Teams with a Week 10 bye: CHI, DEN, PHI, TB. Data pulled on November 16, 2026. Career ratings combine the regular season and the postseason, 1999 to 2026, including the partial 2026 season.

How the ratings work

Each rating combines three inputs from play by play data. For quarterbacks: EPA per dropback, dropback success rate, and sack rate. For running backs: EPA per touch, touch success rate, and fumbles lost per touch. For wide receivers and tight ends: EPA per target, target success rate, and catch rate. Each input is scored against the average and spread of the qualifying players in that table, on a scale where 100 is the average qualifier. The full formula, with weights, thresholds, and limitations, is published on every ratings page.

Seasons covered: 1999 to 2026. Play by play data starts in 1999. No era adjustment is applied, and the rating does not separate a player from teammates, coaching, or scheme. It describes past play value only. It is not a prediction about any future game.

What is not rated here

There are no defensive ratings. The open data has no snap denominators before 2012 and no coverage data, so a defensive formula from this data alone would reward playing time over quality and mix roles that cannot be compared on the same inputs. Shipping a number that cannot be defended would break the promise of this section, so no defensive rating is published. There are no kicking or punting ratings either. Those skills need a different formula design, and they are not part of this build.

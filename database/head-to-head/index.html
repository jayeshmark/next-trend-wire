Coverage is 1999 to 2026. The 2026 season is still in progress, so only scored games through November 15, 2026 are included, and later 2026 meetings are not listed. Franchise mapping: the St. Louis Rams count toward the Los Angeles Rams, the San Diego Chargers count toward the Los Angeles Chargers, and the Oakland Raiders count toward the Las Vegas Raiders. All historical meetings count toward the current franchise.

Editorial Policy

New news only

Next Trend Wire covers news as it happens. It does not republish old stories or reframe anniversaries as news.

Primary sources

Facts come from primary documents: company announcements, government releases, court filings, and official statements. Other news publications are used to discover what is trending, never as the source of a fact. Facts are linked inline to the primary document. Where no primary document can be retrieved, the fact is attributed in the text.

Accuracy

Numbers and facts are checked against the primary document before publication. Where sources conflict, the fact is left out rather than guessed. No claim about a death or a person's health is published without a family, spokesperson, or official statement.

Attribution

Claims that are unproven in public are reported as claims, with the source of the claim named. Allegations are never stated as fact.

Corrections

If you spot an error, contact us and we will check it and correct the article.

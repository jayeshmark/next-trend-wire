Georgia Takes No. 1 as Miami Falls and Ohio State Slides

By Jayesh Marathe · November 11, 2026 · 7 min read

Georgia is the new No. 1 team in the College Football Playoff rankings after the selection committee released its second Top 25 on Tuesday night. The Bulldogs climbed past Miami, which fell to seventh after its first loss of the season, while Ohio State slid all the way to No. 20 in a ranking full of movement.

What happened

  • The new No. 1. Georgia moved up one spot to No. 1 after a 42-7 road win over then-No. 10 Ole Miss on Saturday, a statement victory that also lifted the Bulldogs to the top of the Associated Press Top 25 on Sunday.
  • The fall. Miami dropped from No. 1 to No. 7 after losing to Notre Dame in South Bend on Saturday, its first defeat of a season that had carried the Hurricanes to the top of the first rankings.
  • The second set. The committee released its second of six rankings shows for the 2026-27 season on Tuesday night, with the next update coming November 17 and the final rankings and playoff bracket set for Sunday, December 6.
  • The format. The playoff remains a 12-team field this season, with the top four seeds earning first-round byes and the highest-ranked Group of Six program taking the final spot.

The top 12

  • 1. Georgia (8-1). The Bulldogs are 6-1 in the SEC and return to Sanford Stadium to face No. 22 Missouri on Saturday, November 14, at 3:30 p.m. ET on ABC.
  • 2. Utah (9-0). The unbeaten Utes held their spot behind Georgia, one of two undefeated teams left in the Top 25.
  • 3. Alabama (7-2). The Crimson Tide climbed after a 31-13 road win at then-No. 14 LSU, a statement of its own in Baton Rouge.
  • 4. UCLA (9-0). The unbeaten Bruins stayed in the top four and in line for a first-round bye.
  • 5. Notre Dame (8-1). The Fighting Irish jumped six spots, from No. 11 to No. 5, on the strength of the win over Miami, leaping two spots ahead of the team they just beat.
  • 6. Texas. The Longhorns held a top-six spot and a projected place in the field.
  • 7. Miami (8-1). The Hurricanes fell six spots after one loss, a reminder that the committee punishes a bad week even for a team with one defeat.
  • 8. Tennessee (7-2). The Volunteers moved into the projected bracket, and ESPN's Football Power Index gives them a 36.4 percent chance of making the playoff.
  • 9. Indiana (8-1). The Hoosiers dropped five spots despite not playing last weekend, the sharpest fall in the Top 25 for a team coming off a bye.
  • 10. BYU (7-2). The Cougars stayed in the projected field.
  • 11. Kentucky (7-2). The Wildcats rounded out the SEC-heavy top of the rankings.
  • 12. Texas Tech. The Red Raiders held the last projected at-large spot, with Boise State taking the final place as the best team among the Group of Six.

The movers

  • Ohio State tumbles. The Buckeyes fell to No. 20, outside the playoff picture entirely, a stunning slide for a program that started the season expecting to contend for the title.
  • Oregon climbs. The Ducks rose seven spots, from No. 23 to No. 16, the biggest jump in the rankings.
  • Iowa rises. The Hawkeyes climbed from No. 19 to No. 15, becoming the third-highest-ranked Big Ten team behind UCLA and Indiana, and they need to reach at least No. 11 by December 6 to make the field.
  • Oklahoma State arrives. The Cowboys entered the Top 25 at No. 25 with a 6-3 record, the only new team in the rankings this week.
  • Boise State holds on. The Broncos moved up to No. 24 and remain the lone Group of Six representative in the projected field, which would send them to a first-round matchup against No. 5 Notre Dame.
  • The SEC dominates. Nine SEC teams made the Top 25, and five of them, Georgia, Alabama, Texas, Tennessee, and Kentucky, are projected to make the playoff if the season ended today.

Why it matters

  • One loss is enough. Miami learned what the committee thinks of a single bad Saturday: a six-spot drop after losing to a top-five team, while Georgia's 42-7 win over a top-10 opponent was rewarded with the top spot.
  • The committee rewards the win, not the reputation. Notre Dame jumped six spots for beating Miami, while Indiana fell five spots for doing nothing at all, which tells teams that the idle week is not a safe week.
  • Ohio State is in trouble. The Buckeyes sit at No. 20 with only four weeks left to change the committee's mind, and the margin for error is gone.
  • The Group of Six race is a one-team show. Boise State is the only Group of Six program in the Top 25, which means the final spot in the field is theirs to lose, and every other Group of Six contender is already out of the picture.

What to watch next

  • Georgia hosts Missouri. The Bulldogs face No. 22 Missouri on Saturday with the top spot to defend and a first-round bye on the line.
  • The next rankings. The committee releases its third Top 25 on Tuesday, November 17, and the final bracket is set on December 6, the day after the conference title games.
  • The bracket takes shape. The current projection would pit No. 1 Georgia against the winner of the No. 8 Tennessee and No. 9 Indiana first-round game, a matchup that would put the committee's own seeding to the test.
  • The last dance for 12 teams. The playoff expands to 24 teams next season, so this is the final year of the 12-team format, and the fight for the last few spots is now the story of November.

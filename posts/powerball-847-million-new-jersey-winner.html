A Single New Jersey Ticket Won the $847 Million Powerball Jackpot

By Editorial Team · 7 min read

A single ticket sold in New Jersey won the $847 million Powerball jackpot in the drawing on Wednesday, November 4, matching all six numbers to take the ninth-largest prize in the game's history. The winner can take the full $847 million as an annuity or a $363.1 million lump sum, and both figures are before taxes.

The winning numbers

  • The numbers. The winning numbers were white balls 15, 34, 41, 53, 56 and red Powerball 21, and the Power Play multiplier was two. Powerball announced the win on Thursday, November 5, and the announcement confirmed that one ticket matched all six numbers drawn the night before.
  • The choice. The winner may choose between an annuitized prize of $847 million and a lump-sum payment of $363.1 million. If the winner selects the annuity, the prize arrives in 30 increasing payments over 29 years, so the advertised jackpot is not the same as an immediate cash payout.
  • The odds. Powerball puts the odds of matching all five white balls plus the red Powerball at 1 in 292.2 million, which is why jackpots can roll over for weeks and climb into the hundreds of millions before anyone wins.
  • The ticket holder. Powerball did not name the ticket holder or the store that sold the ticket. The announcement says only that the single winning ticket was sold in New Jersey, and the winner has not come forward in public.

A ninth-largest prize

  • Where it ranks. The $847 million prize is the ninth-largest jackpot in Powerball history. It sits just above the $842.4 million prize won in Michigan on January 1, 2024, and just below the $1.04 billion prize won in Illinois on August 12, 2026.
  • Back-to-back giants. The Illinois win and the New Jersey win rank as the eighth- and ninth-largest prizes in the game's history, and they landed less than three months apart. Two jackpots of this size in such a short span is rare, and it put two new entries on the game's all-time list in quick succession.
  • The top of the list. The biggest Powerball jackpot ever remains the $2.04 billion prize won in California on November 7, 2022. The rest of the top five are a $1.817 billion prize in Arkansas on December 24, 2025, a $1.787 billion prize in Missouri and Texas on September 6, 2025, a $1.765 billion prize in California on October 11, 2023, and a $1.586 billion prize in California, Florida and Tennessee on January 13, 2016.
  • What the game said. Stephen Durrell, Chair of the Powerball Product Group and Executive Director of the Kansas Lottery, said: "Back-to-back jackpot runs resulting in prizes of this magnitude are a testament to the excitement Powerball generates. We're thrilled to celebrate another life-changing win and congratulate the lucky ticket holder."

Who else won

  • More than a million winners. Lotteries are urging players to check their tickets carefully, because more than 1 million tickets won cash prizes in the November 4 drawing by matching one or more of the winning numbers. Most of those prizes are small, but the drawing produced a long list of five-figure winners too.
  • The $50,000 tier. Twenty-five tickets matched four white balls plus the Powerball to win $50,000 each. Those tickets missed the jackpot by a single white ball, and each one still pays out a substantial prize.
  • The $100,000 tier. Four more tickets matched four white balls plus the Powerball and included the Power Play option, so each won $100,000. The Power Play multiplier was two, which doubled those prizes from $50,000 to $100,000.
  • A California note. In California, prize payouts are pari-mutuel, which means they are determined by sales and the number of winners rather than set at a fixed amount. Players in other states receive the fixed prize amounts listed for each tier.

What happens next

  • The reset. The jackpot resets to $20 million for the next drawing on Saturday, November 7. The Power Play 10X multiplier will be available for that drawing, since the advertised jackpot is below $150 million.
  • The 2026 run. The New Jersey win is the seventh Powerball jackpot won in 2026. The earlier winners were a $209.3 million prize in North Carolina on January 21, a $250.8 million prize in Arkansas on March 2, a $230.8 million prize in Delaware on April 6, a $143 million prize in Indiana and Kansas on April 29, a $20 million prize in Florida and Texas on May 2, and the $1.04 billion prize in Illinois on August 12.
  • The wait. The ticket holder now faces the choice between 30 payments spread over 29 years and a single payment of $363.1 million, and until the prize is claimed the biggest open question is who holds the ticket.

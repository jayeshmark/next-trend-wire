geniosAI Emerges From Stealth With $19.1 Million to Make AI Finance Traceable

By Jayesh Marathe · November 9, 2026 · 7 min read

geniosAI has emerged from stealth with $19.1 million in combined seed and Series A funding from FactSet, Zoom and General Catalyst, with the Series A led by FactSet, to build AI that shows financial institutions exactly how it reached every answer.

The round

  • The headline. geniosAI announced $19.1 million in combined seed and Series A funding from FactSet, Zoom and General Catalyst, with the Series A led by FactSet.
  • The lead. FactSet is geniosAI's strategic partner and lead investor, and the two companies are bringing FactSet's financial data together with geniosAI's platform.
  • The backers. Zoom and General Catalyst participated in the round alongside FactSet.
  • The split. geniosAI did not disclose the individual sizes of the seed and Series A rounds or its valuation.
  • The founder. The company was co-founded and is led by chief executive Prabhdeep Singh, known as PD Singh, and is headquartered in San Jose, California.

What geniosAI builds

  • The pitch. geniosAI builds a financial intelligence agent platform for banks, investment teams and the office of the CFO, designed to automate complex analysis while preserving the underlying data, calculations and decision making behind it.
  • The difference. Most AI tools in finance make an individual analyst faster. geniosAI is built for the institution, so the assumptions, methods and review decisions behind every piece of work stay with the firm instead of leaving with the person or the project.
  • The stack. Its AI domain stack combines a financial ontology, agents and traceable orchestration, delivering deep analysis for investment research and modeling with the reasoning shown and every figure traced back to the source it came from.
  • The compounding. Because the work is captured rather than rebuilt, a firm's expertise accumulates instead of resetting with every engagement, so the next assignment starts from what a team already established rather than from nothing.
  • The users. geniosAI is already in use with finance and investment teams at leading Fortune 500 global banks and technology companies to prepare for earnings, including how results compare with peers and analyst expectations, what stands out and the questions analysts are likely to ask.

The partners' case

  • The data. "The value of data is defined by how decisively you can act on it. As AI makes access to intelligence abundant, confidence comes from understanding and trusting where every answer comes from," said Sanoke Viswanathan, chief executive officer of FactSet. "We are bringing FactSet's trusted data together with geniosAI's traceable analysis to help teams move from insight to decision with greater conviction and speed."
  • The founder. "A financial answer is only useful if you can show how you got there. geniosAI keeps the sources, calculations, assumptions, and review decisions attached to the work, so the institution can trace every number, challenge it, and build on it the next time," said Prabhdeep Singh, co-founder and chief executive officer of geniosAI.
  • The Zoom angle. "Zoom's technology enables customers to drive work from conversation to completion," said Abhisht Arora, chief strategy officer at Zoom. "Our investment in geniosAI aligns with our focus on tools that help customers get the job done and builds on our growing strength in the finance function, from internal finance teams to some of the largest global financial institutions."
  • The General Catalyst view. "The bigger opportunity in transforming customer value is delivering a higher level of intelligence and insights faster and with superior quality across the organization," said Madhu Namburi, managing director at General Catalyst. "geniosAI keeps judgment with the business, allowing intelligence to compound, while empowering individuals to apply that higher level of intelligence consistently across the organization."

What comes next

  • The products. geniosAI is bringing products to market for the office of the CFO and for investment professionals on both the buy side and the sell side.
  • The FactSet tie. The partnership puts FactSet's data inside geniosAI's platform, so finance and investment teams get analysis they can trace to its source, review, verify and build on.
  • The Zoom tie. Zoom powers the conversations where financial decisions are made, from internal finance reviews to earnings calls, and geniosAI brings financial intelligence into those conversations.
  • The funding. The new capital will support development of the platform and broader deployment among banks, investment teams and corporate finance organizations.

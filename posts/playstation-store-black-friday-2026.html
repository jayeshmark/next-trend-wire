PlayStation Store Black Friday Deals Open November 4 With Up to 75% Off Thousands of Games

By Jayesh Marathe · November 5, 2026 · 7 min read

Sony's PlayStation Store Black Friday Deals open Wednesday, November 4, with discounts of up to 75% across thousands of PS5, PS4, and PS VR2 games, expansions, and downloadable content, Sony announced on PlayStation.Blog on November 3, 2026. The sale runs through Monday, November 30, refreshes with new titles on November 16, and ends some discounts early on November 20.

The sale

  • The opening. Sony announced that PlayStation's Black Friday Deals kick off early on PlayStation Store, starting Wednesday, November 4, with deep discounts up to 75% off across thousands of PS5, PS4, and PS VR2 games, expansions, and downloadable content.
  • The early start. The sale opens more than three weeks before Black Friday itself, which falls on November 27, giving shoppers nearly a full month of deals instead of a single weekend.
  • The refresh. The promotion refreshes at midnight Pacific, Greenwich Mean Time, and Japan Standard Time on November 16, when new titles join the sale and some existing offers change.
  • The early cutoff. Certain discounts end on November 20 at 11:59 p.m. in those same time zones, before the promotion finishes, and some deals will be replaced with new offers, so a discount visible on opening day may not last until the end of the month.
  • The close. The full promotion ends on November 30 at 11:59 p.m., closing out a sale that runs for 27 days from start to finish.

What is on sale

  • The catalog. The sale covers games, expansions, and downloadable content for PS5, PS4, and PS VR2, spanning thousands of titles rather than a small curated list.
  • The discount ceiling. Sony advertises savings of up to 75%, which is the maximum discount in the promotion, not a promise that every participating game will carry that reduction.
  • The preview. Sony published a small preview of just some of the titles that will be discounted, and said the discounts, along with the full list of games in the promotion, will be revealed on PlayStation Store when the Black Friday Deals begin.
  • The regional catch. Promotions and percentage discounts may differ by region, so the price on a game in one country's store may not match the price in another, and Sony points shoppers to the PlayStation Store Black Friday page for their own region's details.
  • The product page rule. Sony advises shoppers to check each item's product page for more details, since end dates and discount levels vary by title.

What is not in the announcement

  • The hardware gap. The announcement covers games, expansions, and downloadable content only, and it names no discounts on PS5 consoles, controllers, headsets, or other accessories.
  • The membership gap. The announcement also names no PlayStation Plus membership discounts, so anyone waiting on a subscription deal will need to watch for a separate announcement.
  • The price gap. Sony published no individual prices or per game discount percentages in the announcement itself, so the size of each discount only becomes clear once the sale goes live on the store.

How to shop the sale

  • The store page. Visit the PlayStation Store's dedicated Black Friday promotion page when the sale goes live to see the full list of discounted games and regional pricing.
  • The November 16 check. Check back on November 16, when the refresh brings new titles into the sale and replaces some of the opening offers.
  • The November 20 warning. Buy before November 20 if a discount you want carries that earlier end date, because it will not necessarily return before the promotion ends on November 30.
  • The bottom line. The sale rewards early shoppers twice over: the deepest catalog is available from November 4, but the best time to buy any single title is before its own listed end date, not the promotion's final day.

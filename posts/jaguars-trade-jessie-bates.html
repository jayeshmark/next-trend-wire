Jaguars Land Pro Bowl Safety Jessie Bates in Trade Deadline Deal With Falcons

By Editorial Team · 7 min read

The Jacksonville Jaguars acquired Pro Bowl safety Jessie Bates III from the Atlanta Falcons on Monday, sending two future draft picks to Atlanta a day before the NFL trade deadline. The move loads up a defense that already leads the AFC South for a playoff push, and it leaves the Falcons selling after a 3-6 start.

The deal

  • The trade. The Jaguars acquired Bates from the Falcons on Monday in exchange for a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick, with the deal pending a physical.
  • The salary split. Atlanta will pay most of Bates' remaining $6.5 million salary to make the deal work under Jacksonville's tight salary cap.
  • The contract. Bates is in the final year of a four-year, $64.02 million contract he signed with Atlanta in March 2023, and he can become a free agent after the season.
  • The farewell. Bates posted "ATL Forever Grateful!" on social media within an hour of the trade breaking, and the Falcons thanked him "for everything on and off the field."

Why Jacksonville paid up

  • A hole at safety. The Jaguars lost starting safety Eric Murray to a season-ending elbow injury and placed him on injured reserve on November 3, leaving the secondary short.
  • A contender loading up. Jacksonville is 6-2 after a 33-10 win over Baltimore on Thursday night, leading the AFC South by three games and sitting third in the AFC.
  • A proven ball hawk. Bates has started all nine games this season with 60 tackles, one interception, three forced fumbles, and a fumble-return touchdown, and he has 28 career interceptions across nine seasons.
  • The general manager's case. "Jessie has been a proven playmaker on the defensive side of the ball for the last 8+ seasons," general manager James Gladstone said. "His skillset complements our style of play and the person he is aligns with our culture."
  • A possible 18-game season. Bates could play in 18 regular-season games this year, nine for Atlanta and nine for Jacksonville, because the Falcons have yet to take their bye week.

Why Atlanta sold

  • A lost season. The Falcons are 3-6 after a 34-17 loss to Cincinnati in Madrid on Sunday, and they sit a game out of first place in a wide-open NFC South.
  • Picks over a walkaway. With Bates set for free agency, Atlanta chose two draft picks now over the risk of losing him for nothing in March.
  • The next man up. Second-year safety Xavier Watts, who recorded five interceptions as a rookie, is expected to slide into Bates' free safety spot.
  • A clean break. Bates started all 60 games he played for Atlanta, recording 392 tackles, 14 interceptions, and 11 forced fumbles in three seasons with the club.

A second deal the same day

  • More help up front. Hours after landing Bates, the Jaguars agreed to acquire defensive tackle Davon Godchaux from the New Orleans Saints for a 2028 sixth-round pick.
  • The pick can move up. The sixth-rounder becomes a 2027 sixth-round pick if Jacksonville acquires one through another trade or the compensatory formula.
  • Filling another injury gap. Godchaux replaces DaVon Hamilton, who suffered a season-ending torn quadriceps in Week 8, and the 31-year-old has played in all eight Saints games this season.
  • The deadline clock. The NFL trade deadline arrives Tuesday at 4 p.m. ET, and Jacksonville has now made two deals in a day while Atlanta and New Orleans stockpile picks for the future.

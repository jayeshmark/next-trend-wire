Vumedi Turns 100,000 Doctor Videos Into AI Answers

By Jayesh Marathe · November 11, 2026 · 7 min read

Vumedi expanded its AI powered physician learning on November 10, backed by a $104 million Series B growth investment from Oak HC/FT. The company's AI turns a library of more than 100,000 physician led videos and nearly 5,000 podcast episodes into answers for doctors, and physicians spend more than 4.3 million minutes a month learning on the platform.

The announcement

  • What changed. Vumedi announced expanded AI powered learning capabilities that help doctors connect clinical questions with the experience of leading specialists faster. The AI surfaces the most relevant specialist perspectives and synthesizes how multiple experts approach the same problem, with time stamped links to the underlying video or episode.
  • The backing. The expansion builds on a $104 million Series B growth investment from Oak HC/FT, a venture and growth equity firm focused on technology and healthcare. Vumedi used the capital for talent, research and development, product development and outreach.
  • The scale. Vumedi's library holds more than 100,000 physician led videos and nearly 5,000 podcast episodes across more than 21 specialties, with 20,000 new videos added over the past 12 months.
  • The problem it targets. The company says the time for a breakthrough treatment to reach peak adoption in everyday clinical practice has stretched from two to three years to six to seven years, and its AI is built to collapse that timeline.

How doctors use it

  • Ask, then watch the expert. A physician asks a clinical question, and the AI points to the most relevant specialist perspectives instead of making the doctor search through hours of video.
  • Multiple experts, one synthesis. The AI synthesizes how several experts approach the same problem, so a doctor sees where specialists agree and where they differ, with links back to each source video.
  • Adoption is fast. More than 150,000 NPI verified clinicians have used the AI tools since launch less than a year ago, generating more than one million interactions.
  • Engagement jumps. Physicians who use the AI tools spend 3.5 times more time on the platform than non AI users, watch 122 percent more video, and average more than 52 minutes of engagement per active clinician each month.

The numbers behind it

  • Minutes add up. Physicians spent more than 56 million minutes learning on Vumedi in the past 12 months, which the company says equals 39,000 three day medical conferences.
  • Monthly habit. The platform now records more than 4.3 million minutes of learning a month, a figure that keeps growing as the AI tools spread.
  • What the founders say. "AI makes access to real clinical experience more important," said founder and chief executive Roman Giverts, framing the technology as a way to spread what experienced specialists know rather than replace them.
  • What the investor says. Oak HC/FT co-founder and managing partner Andrew Adams is quoted in the announcement backing the expansion, as the firm bets that peer video plus AI can close the gap between new treatments and everyday practice.

Why it matters

  • Peer learning at scale. Vumedi is a peer to peer learning community for physicians, where clinicians learn directly from the experts and peers advancing their field. The AI layer turns that community's video archive into something a doctor can query in the moment.
  • The crowded field. AI medical tools are multiplying, but Vumedi's bet is that answers grounded in named specialists' own recorded experience carry weight that a generic model cannot match.
  • What to watch. The test is whether the engagement gains hold as the tools reach more of the platform's clinicians, and whether time spent learning translates into faster adoption of new treatments in practice.

Mike Tirico Misses Sunday Night Football Over Health Issue as Noah Eagle Fills In

By Jayesh Marathe · November 9, 2026 · 7 min read

NBC play-by-play voice Mike Tirico missed Sunday Night Football on November 8 because of a health issue, hours before the Tampa Bay Buccaneers played the Chicago Bears in Chicago. Noah Eagle stepped in at short notice to call the game, and Tirico said he expects to be back in the booth for next Sunday's game in Cincinnati.

The announcement

  • NBC Sports shared Tirico's statement on Sunday afternoon. Tirico said in the statement that he would miss the game in Chicago due to a health issue, and he looked forward to watching the game from home.
  • He promised a quick return. "I look forward to watching tonight and can't wait to be back with Cris, Melissa and the rest of our SNF team next Sunday night in Cincinnati," Tirico said in the statement.
  • Neither Tirico nor NBC gave details about the issue. The statement did not describe the health problem, and the network disclosed nothing more about it.
  • It was the first Sunday Night Football assignment he has missed. Tirico has been the lead voice of the telecast since the 2022 season, when he replaced Al Michaels, and the 2026 season is his fifth in the role.

The fill-in

  • Noah Eagle called the game with Cris Collinsworth and Melissa Stark. NBC named Eagle, its designated backup for major NFL broadcasts, as the replacement in the same announcement.
  • Eagle came straight from another prime-time game. The 29-year-old had called Notre Dame's win over top-ranked Miami in South Bend on Saturday night, then traveled to Chicago to take the Sunday night booth with almost no preparation.
  • Eagle leads NBC's college football coverage. He is the network's lead play-by-play announcer for college football and has built a rapid rise since joining NBC permanently in 2023, including NFL playoff games and Olympic basketball.
  • The Bears won the game Eagle called. Chicago beat Tampa Bay 27-24 at Soldier Field, in a game fans kept noting felt different without Tirico's familiar voice.

A milestone delayed

  • The game was scheduled to be Tirico's 300th NFL broadcast. The milestone will have to wait a week, resetting for the Week 10 game between the Cincinnati Bengals and the Pittsburgh Steelers.
  • That game airs next Sunday night from Cincinnati. Kickoff is set for 8:20 p.m. ET on November 15, and Tirico said he expects to be back with the Sunday Night Football team for it.
  • Tirico has a record of broadcasting through pain. He called a November 2024 game days after tearing his Achilles tendon, which is part of why a missed assignment stood out to viewers.

What is known and what is not

  • What is known is limited to the statement. Tirico missed one game for a health issue, Eagle filled in, and Tirico says he will return on November 15.
  • What is not known is the nature of the issue. No details have been disclosed, and nothing in the announcement suggests it is serious, since Tirico is already talking about returning next week.
  • The booth returns to normal next Sunday if he is back. The Week 10 matchup in Cincinnati will reunite Tirico with Collinsworth and Stark, and the delayed 300th broadcast will be the subplot of the night.

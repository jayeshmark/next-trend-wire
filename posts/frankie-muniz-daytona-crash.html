Frankie Muniz Hospitalized After Daytona Practice Crash

By Jayesh Marathe · November 12, 2026 · 7 min read

Actor and NASCAR driver Frankie Muniz was taken to a hospital on Wednesday after crashing during practice at Daytona International Speedway. Ford Racing said the 40-year-old was "awake and alert" after the crash, which happened hours before the exhibition race he was due to drive in.

The crash

  • The incident. Muniz crashed his Ford Mustang Dark Horse R during a practice session on Wednesday, November 11, at Daytona International Speedway, ahead of Ford's Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic. The track was not open to the public at the time.
  • The statement. Ford Racing said in a statement, "Frankie Muniz was involved in an incident while practicing for today's Proud to Honor event at Daytona International Speedway. He was transported to a local medical facility for further evaluation and is awake and alert."
  • The car. Photos from the scene showed heavy damage to the race car, with the roof cut away by rescue workers to extract Muniz. The extent of his injuries has not been disclosed.
  • The cause. Ford Racing reviewed the car's data after the crash. "The team here at Ford Racing looked through all the data and didn't find anything happened to the car (before the crash)," driver Brad Keselowski told reporters.

The race he will miss

  • The event. The Proud to Honor Veterans Day Classic, an exhibition race at Daytona's road course, was set for 8 p.m. ET on Wednesday on USA Network, with 12 drivers paired into six teams. The event benefits six nonprofits that support military and veteran communities through the Proud to Honor Fund.
  • The team. Muniz was set to partner with Cup Series driver Austin Cindric on a team representing the Air Force. Ford development driver Jenson Altzman, 24, who competes in IMSA, will replace him.
  • The format. The race is the first of its kind, built to showcase Ford's top drivers while honoring the military, with each team representing a branch of the armed forces.

The driver

  • The actor. Muniz, 40, rose to fame as a child star on the Fox series Malcolm in the Middle, and recently returned to television in Malcolm in the Middle: Life's Unfair, an update of the classic series.
  • The racer. He now races full time in the NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series, driving the No. 33 Ford for Team Reaume. In October 2026 he was crowned the GT4 America Am champion, his first professional motorsports championship.
  • The future. Muniz is expected to continue racing in both the Truck Series and the GT4 America Series in 2027.

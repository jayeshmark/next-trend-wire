Madonna Takes the Dance Tent as Coachella Reveals 2027 Headliners

By Jayesh Marathe · November 11, 2026 · 7 min read

Coachella revealed its 2027 lineup, with Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA headlining the two weekends in Indio, California, and Madonna billed in the dance tent on Friday. The festival returns to the Empire Polo Club on April 9 to 11 and April 16 to 18, 2027, and passes go on sale Friday, November 13 at noon Pacific time.

The headliners

  • Friday belongs to Dua Lipa. Dua Lipa headlines Friday, April 9 and April 16, topping a day that also features Madonna in the dance tent, Zara Larsson, Steve Lacy, JISOO, Diljit Dosanjh, Peggy Gou and Kehlani.
  • Saturday belongs to Tame Impala. Tame Impala headlines Saturday, April 10 and April 17, with Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer, Tiesto, Martin Garrix, Robyn, Maneskin and NCT on the same day.
  • Sunday belongs to SZA. SZA closes the festival on Sunday, April 11 and April 18, joined by Don Toliver, Dom Dolla, Kygo, Four Tet, Becky G and BABYMONSTER.
  • The fourth billing. Daddy Yankee gets a billing of his own, "In the Desert," a special production separate from the three day headliners, after returning to music last year following a farewell tour in 2023.

The surprises in the poster

  • Madonna in the dance tent. Madonna returns to the festival more than two decades after her last Coachella set, and Goldenvoice placed her in the dance tent rather than on the main stage, a booking that puts one of pop's biggest names on the second line of the poster.
  • Diljit Dosanjh returns. Diljit Dosanjh is back on the Friday lineup after his 2023 Coachella debut, when he became the first Punjabi artist to perform at the festival.
  • K-pop's new wave. JISOO plays her first solo Coachella set on Friday, while NCT and BABYMONSTER make their Coachella debuts on Saturday and Sunday. Five Korean acts appear across the weekend, including Peggy Gou and the traditional band Inalchi.
  • Justice in the Bunker. Justice will debut "Blinded by the Lights" in the Bunker, a new staging for the French duo's return to the desert.

The rest of the bill

  • Friday's depth. The Friday lineup runs from pop to electronic to global sounds, with Giorgio Moroder, Leon Thomas, Amelie Lens, Sara Landry and Remi Wolf among the names on the poster.
  • Saturday's range. Saturday pairs indie acts like Phoebe Bridgers and Weezer with dance headliners Tiesto and Martin Garrix, plus Robyn, Maneskin, Role Model and Adela.
  • Sunday's close. Sunday's bill leans electronic and Latin, with Kygo, Empire of the Sun, The Neighbourhood, Victoria Monet and Becky G supporting SZA's closing set.
  • Two weekends, one poster. The same lineup plays both weekends, April 9 to 11 and April 16 to 18, at the Empire Polo Club in Indio.

How to go

  • Passes go on sale Friday. Passes go on sale Friday, November 13 at noon Pacific time through the festival's official website, with hotel packages and bundles also available.
  • The wait is short. The lineup reveal lands five months before the festival, giving fans three days to decide before the on sale opens.
  • What to watch. The open question is which of the three headliners draws the biggest crowd, and whether Madonna's dance tent set becomes the weekend's most talked about performance.

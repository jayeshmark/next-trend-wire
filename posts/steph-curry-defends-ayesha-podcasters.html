Steph Curry Calls Out Podcasters Who Attacked Wife Ayesha

By Jayesh Marathe · November 12, 2026 · 7 min read

Stephen Curry has publicly called out former NBA players Gilbert Arenas and Nick Young for criticizing his wife Ayesha over a kissing scene in her new film, saying the attacks stepped on her accomplishments. Within hours, both men apologized, and so did former NFL player Emmanuel Acho.

The callout

  • The video. Curry posted a 90-second video to Instagram on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, naming Arenas, Young and Acho and telling them to stop offering opinions on his wife's career.
  • The line. "I tried to laugh this one off, but something needs to be said," Curry said in the video, adding that the criticism spoke to a bigger conversation about how women are viewed and what they are allowed to do.
  • The demand. Curry told the three men that when Ayesha's name appears in a headline, the congratulations and the apology should be as loud as the criticism.
  • The timing. The video came while the Warriors were on an eight-game winning streak, with Curry in the middle of a fast start to the season.

What set it off

  • The film. Ayesha Curry stars as Chelsey, a dating expert who reconnects with her high school sweetheart, in Second Chance Holiday, a romantic comedy that premiered on the Roku Channel on November 5. It is her first leading role in a feature film.
  • The scene. A clip of Ayesha sharing an on-screen kiss with co-star Matthew Daddario spread on social media and drew debate on several sports podcasts.
  • The podcast. On their show We Said What We Said, Arenas called Ayesha "the only blemish in the family" and said she does not behave like the wives of other NBA superstars, comparing her unfavorably with Savannah James and Cookie Johnson.
  • The pile-on. Young joined the discussion on the same show, and former NFL linebacker Emmanuel Acho and former Eagles running back LeSean McCoy also criticized the kiss scene on their own podcasts.

The apologies

  • The speed. Within about an hour of Curry's video, Arenas and Young issued apologies on their podcast, Gil's Arena.
  • Young's words. Young said, "I apologize like a man. I'm sorry, Steph. You my dog. I love you. If I said anything, I apologize like a man. I'm sorry."
  • Arenas's words. Arenas said, "I'm sorry if he's offended," and added that he had not meant disrespect.
  • Acho follows. Acho apologized hours later on his Speakeasy show.
  • Ayesha responds. Ayesha thanked Steph on Instagram after a screening of the film, writing that he always makes her feel like the luckiest girl in the world.

Why it matters

  • The rare rebuke. Curry almost never answers critics publicly, and his decision to name three men by name turned a podcast argument into a sports-media flashpoint.
  • The point. Curry framed the episode as a question about how women married to famous athletes are treated, not just a defense of his wife.
  • The free publicity. Curry even turned the criticism into promotion for the film, thanking the podcasters for the free publicity and encouraging their audiences to watch Second Chance Holiday.
  • The apology test. All three men apologized within a day, which is the outcome Curry asked for: an apology as loud as the criticism.

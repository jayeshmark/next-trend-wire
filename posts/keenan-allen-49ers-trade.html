49ers Trade Sixth Round Pick for Six Time Pro Bowler Keenan Allen

By Jayesh Marathe · November 10, 2026 · 7 min read

The San Francisco 49ers acquired wide receiver Keenan Allen from the Indianapolis Colts on November 10 for a 2027 sixth round pick, adding a veteran pass catcher hours before the NFL trade deadline to a receiving room hit hard by injuries.

The deal

  • The terms. The 49ers receive Allen, and the Colts receive a 2027 sixth round pick, in a deal reported by the Ringer's Tom Pelissero on Tuesday afternoon.
  • The condition. The deal is contingent on Allen passing a physical with the 49ers, the standard final step before a deadline trade becomes official.
  • The timing. The teams agreed to the trade hours before the 4 p.m. ET deadline, as the 7-1 49ers moved to fix their biggest position of need before the cutoff.
  • The pattern. It is the second trade between the teams in recent weeks, after San Francisco acquired defensive tackle DeForest Buckner from Indianapolis on October 27.

Why the 49ers needed him

  • The injuries. San Francisco's receiver room has been battered all season, with Mike Evans out with an Achilles injury and Deebo Samuel dealing with a hamstring injury, and Christian Kirk is gone as well.
  • The record. Despite the injuries, the 49ers sit at 7-1 and lead the NFC West, and Allen gives quarterback Brock Purdy a reliable target who can keep drives going.
  • The debut. Allen is on track to be available when the 49ers play at the Dallas Cowboys on November 15, and he could step into the starting lineup right away.
  • The room. He joins a receiver group that includes Evans, Samuel, Demarcus Robinson, Brandin Cooks, Jacob Cowing and Jordan Watkins, adding experience to a young and injured unit.

The player

  • The season. Allen, 34, has caught 40 passes for 351 yards and one touchdown in eight games for the Colts this season, leading Indianapolis in receptions.
  • The career. A six time Pro Bowl selection, Allen has caught 1,095 passes for 12,402 yards and 71 touchdowns, the eighth most receptions in NFL history.
  • The journey. He spent 12 seasons with the San Diego and Los Angeles Chargers, the team that drafted him in the third round in 2013, and the 49ers are his fourth team in three seasons.
  • The contract. Allen signed a one year deal with the Colts in August, and Indianapolis turned a late offseason signing into a draft pick by selling him at the deadline.

The Colts fire sale

  • The second sale of the day. Allen left Indianapolis hours before teammate Josh Downs was traded to the Kansas City Chiefs, and the two deals capped a deadline day sell off for the 2-7 Colts.
  • The reset. The Colts have now traded four starters this season, with guard Quenton Nelson and Buckner going in October, after firing general manager Chris Ballard and turning toward a rebuild.
  • The return. Indianapolis turned Allen, a player it signed off the street in August, into a 2027 sixth round pick, a small but real return for a veteran on a one year deal.

What comes next

  • The physical. Allen must pass his physical with San Francisco before the deal is final, and the 49ers will then work him into the offense ahead of the Dallas game.
  • The stretch. The 49ers paid almost nothing for a proven receiver, and if Allen stays healthy, the deal could look like one of the best value moves of deadline day.
  • The Colts. Indianapolis is left with a stripped down roster and a pile of draft picks, and the rest of the season belongs to the rebuild.

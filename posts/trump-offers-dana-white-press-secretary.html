Trump Offers UFC Chief Dana White the White House Press Secretary Job

By Editorial Team · 7 min read

President Donald Trump has offered the White House press secretary job to Dana White, the president and chief executive of the UFC, in a post on Truth Social on Saturday. Trump asked White to bring six mixed martial arts fighters with him as assistants, and it remains unclear whether the offer was serious or a joke.

The offer

  • Trump made the offer on Truth Social. Trump wrote on Saturday that Dana White "has been offered the job of Press Secretary of the United States", calling White "a violent Fighter and Warrior" in a post on his Truth Social account.
  • He asked White to bring six fighters as assistants. Trump said he had asked White to bring Islam Makhachev, Khabib Nurmagomedov, Justin Gaethje, Daniel Cormier, Amanda Nunes and Kayla Harrison as assistants, and noted that some of them are not from the United States.
  • One name came out wrong. The post spelled the last fighter's name "Kayla Harris," while the UFC champion's name is Kayla Harrison.
  • The fighters would defend against the left. "We need these people to defend us against the Lunatic Left," Trump wrote. "Dana will be a Press Secretary like none other. Dana, please take the job!"
  • The post carried photos of the fighters. Trump posted photos of the six fighters with the announcement.
  • White played along on Instagram. White posted a photo of himself standing at the White House briefing room podium on his Instagram Stories, with the caption "On to the next chapter."

Is the offer real?

  • CNN reported that White declined. CNN reported, citing a source familiar with the matter, that White had declined the offer.
  • An aide said Trump was joking. One aide told CNN that Trump was joking about the offer.
  • White dodged the question on Saturday. Asked about the offer at a news conference on Saturday, White said only that he plans to fly to Washington to meet Trump on Monday.
  • The White House communications director cheered it on. Steven Cheung, the White House communications director, reposted Trump's message and wrote, "Hell yeah! Do it, @DanaWhite!"
  • Neither side has confirmed anything on the record. The White House and the UFC have not commented on the record about whether the offer was serious.
  • The job needs no Senate vote. The press secretary job does not require Senate confirmation, so a yes from White would be all it takes for him to start.

Why the job is open

  • The lectern has sat empty since mid-October. The job has been vacant since Katie Zacharia left after five days in the role in October.
  • Leavitt left earlier in the year. Karoline Leavitt departed the role earlier in the year, leaving Trump searching for a permanent replacement.
  • Trump and White go back years. White is a longtime Trump supporter, and the UFC staged a fight card on the White House lawn in June for the country's 250th anniversary celebrations.
  • Miller just took the top staff job. Trump named Stephen Miller as White House chief of staff on Friday, so the press secretary search is the second staffing question of the weekend.

What happens next

  • White meets Trump on Monday. White said he plans to fly to Washington to meet Trump on Monday, which should settle whether the offer was real.
  • The search goes on if White says no. If White declines, Trump will keep looking for a press secretary three weeks after the job fell open.
  • No official word has settled it. As of Saturday night, neither Trump nor the White House had said whether the post was serious or a joke.

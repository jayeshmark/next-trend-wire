Grand Theft Auto VI Pre-Loading Opens Today Ahead of November 19 Launch

By Jayesh Marathe · November 10, 2026 · 7 min read

Grand Theft Auto VI pre-loading opens today, November 10, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, nine days before the game launches on November 19. Rockstar Games set the download at up to 212 GB, so players need to clear space now if they want to play the moment the game unlocks.

The pre-load times

  • PlayStation 5. Digital pre-loading begins at midnight local time on Tuesday, November 10, in each player's time zone, according to Rockstar's announcement.
  • Xbox Series X|S. Pre-loading begins globally at 12 a.m. GMT on Tuesday, November 10, which put the start at 7 p.m. ET and 4 p.m. PT on Monday, November 9, for players in the United States.
  • Code in box. Physical editions ship as a download code in the box, and Rockstar says those versions should be available from retailers beginning November 12.
  • The unlock. Pre-loading only downloads the game. It stays locked until launch day, when it unlocks at midnight local time on November 19.

The 212 GB download

  • The size. Rockstar says the pre-load file will require up to 212 GB depending on the device, one of the largest downloads ever asked of console players.
  • The warning. Rockstar told players to make sure they have ample space before starting the download, since a file that large will not fit on a nearly full drive.
  • The head start. The November 10 start is two days earlier than the November 12 date Rockstar originally set, giving players a nine day window to fetch the game before launch.
  • The platforms. The game launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a PC release date.

The launch and the extras

  • Launch day. Grand Theft Auto VI launches Thursday, November 19, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
  • The setting. The game returns to Vice City and the state of Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida, which players can explore in the Only in Leonida section of the official site.
  • The poster and map. Rockstar will add a free printable cover art poster and a map of Leonida to the official site on November 19, and a double-sided physical version will be available shortly after launch from the Rockstar Store for $0.99 on its own or free with any purchase.
  • The soundtrack. Players can already listen to in-game radio station previews and singles from Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on the official site, along with official videos including An Extended Look.

Take-Two counts down

  • The earnings. Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar's parent company, reported its fiscal second quarter 2027 results after the market closed on Monday, November 9.
  • The quote. "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away and, with pre-orders continuing to reach unprecedented levels, we expect to deliver an historic year for our company," said Strauss Zelnick, chairman and chief executive of Take-Two.
  • The quarter. Total net bookings fell 18 percent to $1.60 billion, compared with $1.96 billion a year earlier, while GAAP net revenue was $1.50 billion and the GAAP net loss narrowed to $54.0 million, or $0.29 per share.
  • The outlook. Take-Two reiterated its fiscal 2027 outlook for net bookings of $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion and forecast $3.35 billion to $3.50 billion in net bookings for the fiscal third quarter, which includes the launch.
  • The date holds. Take-Two's release still lists Grand Theft Auto VI for November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no change to the date or the platforms.

What is still unannounced

  • No PC version. Rockstar has announced the game only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S and has not confirmed a PC version or a PC date.
  • No pre-order number. Take-Two said pre-orders are reaching unprecedented levels but gave no pre-order figure, so third party estimates remain estimates.
  • The stakes. The guidance shows how much rides on the launch. Take-Two expects $3.35 billion to $3.50 billion in net bookings in the quarter that includes it, more than double the $1.60 billion it just reported.
  • The countdown. With pre-loading open and ten days to launch at the time of Take-Two's results, the wait is now measured in days, not months.

Rockstar Confirms GTA 6 Is 30fps on Every Console, Even PS5 Pro

By Jayesh Marathe · November 10, 2026 · 7 min read

Rockstar Games confirmed on November 10 that Grand Theft Auto VI targets 30 frames per second on every console, ending months of speculation that the PlayStation 5 Pro would run the game at a higher frame rate with a performance mode of its own.

The confirmation

  • The line. Rockstar said the game was built from the ground up for current consoles, "targeting 30 frames per second while pushing both platforms to their absolute limits", in a Newswire post published as pre-loading opened on November 10.
  • The Pro. The PlayStation 5 Pro targets the same 30 frames per second as the base PlayStation 5 and both Xbox consoles, and Rockstar described no performance mode, no 60fps mode and no 40fps mode for any console.
  • The wait. The frame rate question has followed the game since its first trailer, with many players expecting Sony's stronger console to hit 40 or even 60 frames per second on the strength of its upgraded hardware.
  • The answer. Instead of a higher frame rate, the Pro's extra power goes into image quality, and the PC version, the only version that could run above 30 frames per second, remains unannounced.

What the PS5 Pro gets

  • The upscaling. The PlayStation 5 Pro offers improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution, Sony's machine learning upscaler, which rebuilds a sharper image from a lower internal resolution.
  • The shadows. Ray traced shadows are available only on PlayStation 5 Pro, drawn from the light actually in the scene instead of baked approximations.
  • The extras. The PlayStation 5 version also includes high fidelity haptic feedback through the DualSense controller, improved spatial audio through Tempest 3D Audio, and improved load times, which Rockstar says make the game play best on PlayStation 5 consoles.

The cloud surprise

  • The Xbox One. Grand Theft Auto VI will be playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, starting at 1 a.m. ET on November 19, so the game reaches the previous generation console by streaming.
  • The settings. Cloud play uses Xbox Series S tier graphical settings, and Rockstar warns that connection speeds and other technical factors can affect streamed performance in ways that would not affect local play.
  • The purchase. Starting November 11, players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime to stream supported games without a Game Pass subscription, and more Rockstar titles are coming to the cloud service, including Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2.

The launch

  • The preload. Pre-loading is now live on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S for players who pre-ordered digitally, with the download taking up to 212 GB of free space.
  • The date. Grand Theft Auto VI launches Thursday, November 19, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, unlocking at midnight local time in each region.
  • The demand. The game is the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store, and the standard edition costs $79.99.
  • The party. Rockstar will celebrate the launch with Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, a live event on November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, Florida.

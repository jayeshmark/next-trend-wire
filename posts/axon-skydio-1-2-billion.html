Axon Puts $1.2 Billion Into Skydio and Becomes Its Exclusive Public Safety Distributor

By Jayesh Marathe · November 8, 2026 · 7 min read

Axon is investing an additional $1.2 billion in Skydio, lifting its fully diluted ownership in the drone maker to 37 percent and becoming the exclusive distributor of Skydio drones to public safety agencies in the United States and Canada. Axon announced the investment on November 5, 2026, in a filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission, calling it the largest single investment in the company's history.

The deal

  • The investment. Axon will invest approximately $1.2 billion in Skydio at a $5.2 billion pre-money valuation, adding about 20 percent to its fully diluted ownership and taking its total stake to 37 percent.
  • The payment. The investment consists of a mix of cash and Axon stock, subject to final transaction terms, and includes both primary capital for Skydio and the purchase of shares from existing Skydio shareholders.
  • The purpose. A portion of the cash will provide growth capital to accelerate innovation, expand Skydio's product portfolio, and bring new drone capabilities to customers faster, according to the announcement.
  • The closing. The transactions that make up the investment are expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions.
  • The scale. Axon called the investment the largest single investment in its history.

The distribution deal

  • The exclusivity. Axon will become Skydio's exclusive distributor for public safety in the United States and Canada, so every Skydio drone sold to a police department or other public safety agency in those countries will now move through Axon.
  • The customers. Skydio already serves more than 1,500 public safety customers, and more than 28 million Americans now live within two miles of a Skydio Drone as First Responder dock.
  • The results. At agencies that deploy Skydio's Drone as First Responder program, priority response times have fallen by as much as 65 percent, with drones arriving before officers on roughly 80 percent of the calls where they are deployed.
  • The market. Axon estimates the total addressable market for Drone as First Responder at approximately $13 billion in the United States and Canada alone, and says the opportunity is now fully accessible through the partnered offering as thousands of agencies begin to evaluate and deploy the technology.

Why Axon is betting this big

  • The chief executive's case. "Within a decade, millions of drones will be working across America. Skydio is positioned to lead in every sector they touch," said Rick Smith, founder and chief executive of Axon. "Axon is taking a major stake in one of the most valuable categories in tech, and Skydio stays free to move fast."
  • The Skydio view. "Axon has been an incredible partner in bringing our drones to public safety customers across the U.S.," said Adam Bry, chief executive of Skydio. "This investment strengthens our ability to accelerate our technology roadmap, scale our impact in public safety and continue advancing autonomous flight across every critical industry we serve."
  • The momentum. Skydio's business is growing rapidly and expects to reach approximately $1 billion in total bookings in its current fiscal year, according to the announcement.
  • The fit. The deal builds on a partnership that is already putting Skydio drones into police departments, and Axon says the expanded program will widen its lead in public safety drones as Skydio builds what Smith called the future of American aerial robotics.

What Skydio is

  • The company. Skydio is the largest United States based drone manufacturer and a world leader in flying robots, founded in 2014 and headquartered in San Mateo, California.
  • The technology. Skydio builds drones around AI backed autonomy that makes them easier to operate, safer to fly, and more capable in complex environments, with software that lets a drone fly itself to a call and stream video back to officers.
  • The reach. Skydio systems are trusted by more than 4,000 agencies and organizations across the United States in the public safety and critical infrastructure sectors, every branch of the United States military, and the armed forces of 32 allied nations.
  • The American angle. Skydio designs, assembles, and supports its products in the United States, a point Axon stressed as police departments look for alternatives to Chinese made drones.

Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 With Faster Output and Lower Prices

By Jayesh Marathe · November 9, 2026 · 7 min read

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 on November 9, its most capable AI models yet, promising faster responses, stronger coding and science results, and lower prices just over two months after the 5.1 versions arrived and ahead of a planned IPO.

What launched

  • Two names, one model. Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 are the same underlying model, released in two configurations with different safeguards.
  • The general version. Claude Fable 5.2 is available to everyone, with strong cybersecurity and biology safeguards that block most requests in those areas, which is what lets Anthropic offer it broadly.
  • The restricted version. Claude Mythos 5.2 runs without some of those safeguards, so access is limited to organizations approved through Anthropic's Cyber Verification Program or Life Sciences Verification Program, while Enterprise customers can reach its capabilities through Claude Security.
  • Where to get it. Fable 5.2 is available today on all platforms, including Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure, and developers can start using it on the Claude Platform as claude-fable-5-2, alongside Claude's paid plans and Claude Code.
  • A launch sweetener. Anthropic is giving subscribers on eligible paid plans extra Fable usage this week, and Fable is included for up to 50 percent of weekly usage limits for Pro plans and standard seats on the Team plan.

The benchmarks

  • Coding. On FrontierCode 2.0, an agentic coding benchmark, Fable 5.2 scored 49.1 percent at its highest effort level, up from 32.4 percent for Fable 5.1, which Anthropic calls the highest score of any released model it has tested.
  • Science. On Terminal-Bench-Science, an agentic scientific research benchmark, Fable 5.2 scored 74.6 percent against 52.6 percent for its predecessor, a jump of 22 points.
  • Cybersecurity. On the CVE Rediscovery benchmark, which tests finding known software vulnerabilities, Mythos 5.2 scored 89.6 percent against 78.3 percent for Mythos 5.1.
  • Agentic coding. On FrontierSWE v2, Fable 5.2 scored 69.9 percent, ahead of Fable 5.1 at 56.3 percent and ahead of Opus 5.5 at 62.3 percent.
  • Clearer answers. Anthropic says testers found that both models communicate more clearly than the 5.1 versions, and generate output up to 30 percent faster.

The price cut

  • Cheaper tokens. Both models now cost $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, down from $10 and $50 for the 5.1 versions.
  • A fast mode. An optional fast mode speeds up token generation by up to 2.5 times, priced at $16 per million input tokens and $80 per million output tokens, and is available on Claude Code, the Claude Platform, Google Cloud and Microsoft Foundry.
  • Undercutting rivals. The new standard rates sit 20 percent below the published prices of OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, which lists $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens.
  • The positioning. Anthropic still recommends that most users start with Claude Opus 5.5 for everyday coding and knowledge work, and move to Fable or Mythos only for the most ambitious, open ended technical work.

Why it matters

  • The IPO backdrop. The launch comes as Anthropic strengthens its position in the fast growing AI market ahead of a planned IPO, and follows the rollout of Claude Haiku 5.5 in October.
  • The race. Fable 5.2 arrives as OpenAI, Google and Anthropic trade the lead on coding and reasoning benchmarks, with each release trying to claim the top spot for the hardest technical work.
  • The safety split. By shipping one model in two versions, Anthropic is drawing a bright line between general purpose AI, which anyone can use, and frontier capabilities in cybersecurity and biology, which only vetted organizations can touch.
  • The system card. Anthropic published a full system card for Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 alongside the launch, with details of its safety testing and evaluation methods.

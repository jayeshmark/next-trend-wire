Bills Beat Vikings 23-6 as Carson Wentz Gets Benched in Prime Time

By Jayesh Marathe · November 9, 2026 · 7 min read

The Buffalo Bills beat the Minnesota Vikings 23-6 on Monday Night Football, and the Vikings benched quarterback Carson Wentz in the fourth quarter after one of the worst starts of his career. Wentz completed 8 of 25 passes for 81 yards with two interceptions before second year pro Max Brosmer finished the game.

The night Wentz lost the job

  • The start. Wentz started in place of Kyler Murray, who was out with a hamstring injury he suffered in Week 8 at Detroit, and he struggled from the opening minutes.
  • The line. Wentz finished 8 of 25 passing for 81 yards and two interceptions, a passer rating of 8.9, and he also lost a fumble on an overhand throw to Justin Jefferson that went behind the line of scrimmage and was recovered by Greg Rousseau at the Minnesota 44.
  • The benching. Coach Kevin O'Connell pulled Wentz in the fourth quarter with Minnesota down three scores, and Brosmer entered and went 6 of 7 for 58 yards with a passer rating of 101.1, though the drive ended on downs.
  • The third downs. Minnesota went 0 for 8 on third down while Buffalo went 7 for 18, and the Bills held the ball for 42 minutes and 32 seconds against Minnesota's 17 minutes and 28 seconds.
  • The damage. Buffalo outgained Minnesota 339 yards to 178 and ran 79 plays to Minnesota's 46. Minnesota trailed only 10-6 at halftime before Buffalo pulled away in the second half.

The quarterback problem

  • Murray out. Murray hurt his hamstring in Week 8 at Detroit, and Wentz was the starter in his place. Murray's return date is unclear, and Brosmer is the only other quarterback on the depth chart.
  • The deadline. The NFL trade deadline falls at 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, November 10, per the league's calendar, leaving Minnesota hours to decide whether to add a quarterback.
  • The record. The loss dropped Minnesota to 6-2, its second defeat of the season, while Buffalo improved to 7-1.
  • The trend. The defeat was Minnesota's second straight loss after a 6-0 start, and it knocked the Vikings out of first place in the NFC North.

Beckham debuts, Peterson honored

  • The signing. The Vikings signed wide receiver Odell Beckham Jr. to the active roster on Monday afternoon, hours before kickoff, after he spent the past month on the practice squad.
  • The moves. Minnesota also placed rookie running back Demond Claiborne on injured reserve and elevated safety Tavierre Thomas from the practice squad.
  • The path. Beckham signed to the practice squad on October 7 after the Giants released him, and Monday's move put him on the 53-man roster for the first time.
  • The honor. Adrian Peterson was formally inducted into the Vikings Ring of Honor during halftime, with his name and No. 28 unveiled and fellow members Cris Carter, Randy Moss and John Randle welcoming him on stage.

What comes next

  • The schedule. The Vikings head to Lambeau Field in Week 10 to face the Green Bay Packers on a short week.
  • The question. Minnesota must now decide whether Wentz keeps the job, Murray returns in time, or the front office finds help before the deadline passes.
  • The stakes. An offense that managed 178 yards in prime time leaves Minnesota with a quarterback decision to make, and the next answer has to come fast.

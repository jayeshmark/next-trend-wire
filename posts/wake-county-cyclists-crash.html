One Cyclist Killed and More Than a Dozen Hurt as Suspected Impaired Driver Plows Into North Carolina Group Ride

By Editorial Team · 7 min read

A 63 year old cyclist was killed and more than a dozen riders were hurt on Saturday morning when a suspected impaired driver crossed the center line and plowed into a Capital Cycling Club group ride in Wake County, North Carolina. The North Carolina State Highway Patrol arrested the 76 year old driver at the scene and charged him with felony death by vehicle and multiple counts tied to the injuries and to leaving the scene.

What happened

  • The crash. The crash happened just after 9:30 a.m. on Saturday, November 7, on Optimist Farm Road near Talicud Trail, about 14 miles southwest of Raleigh. A black Infiniti crossed the center line and drove into a group of about 30 Capital Cycling Club riders, the Highway Patrol said.
  • The driver kept going. The car did not stop after striking the riders. It continued for about half a mile before running off the left side of the road into a ditch, according to the Highway Patrol.
  • The victim. Patrick Allen Turner, 63, of Cary, North Carolina, died at the scene, the Highway Patrol announced. Turner was riding with the club on its regular Saturday morning group ride.
  • The injured. At least a dozen other riders were hurt, and seven of them had serious injuries that sent them to regional trauma centers, Holly Springs Fire Chief LeRoy Smith said. Other riders were treated at WakeMed Raleigh, WakeMed Cary, and UNC Health Holly Springs.
  • The scale. Authorities classified the crash as a mass casualty incident, and emergency crews from multiple agencies responded to the two lane road outside Holly Springs.

The driver and the charges

  • The arrest. Troopers arrested the driver, Donald Robert Morris-Jones, 76, of Apex, North Carolina, at the scene and booked him into the Wake County Detention Center.
  • The charges. Morris-Jones faces felony death by vehicle, seven counts of felony serious injury by vehicle, eight counts of felony hit and run involving serious injury or death, driving while impaired, reckless driving, and driving left of center.
  • The bond. He is being held on a $1 million secured bond, and court records show he is scheduled to appear in court on Monday afternoon, November 9.
  • The impairment question. The Highway Patrol said impairment is suspected to have been a factor in the crash. A patrol spokesperson said the agency cannot speculate on whether alcohol, drugs, or medication caused the impairment, and that the final crash report will answer those questions.

What happens next

  • The investigation. The Highway Patrol is leading the investigation into how the car crossed the center line and why the driver kept driving after striking the group.
  • The club. The Capital Cycling Club, based in the Apex area, is one of the oldest cycling clubs in North Carolina and organizes weekly group rides on Wednesdays and Saturdays.
  • The road. Optimist Farm Road is a wooded two lane route near the NC 540 toll highway, and the crash happened in the 4200 block near Talicud Trail, just south of the toll road.

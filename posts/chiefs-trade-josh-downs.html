Chiefs Trade Two Picks for Josh Downs to Give Mahomes a Weapon

By Jayesh Marathe · November 10, 2026 · 7 min read

The Kansas City Chiefs acquired wide receiver Josh Downs from the Indianapolis Colts on November 10, minutes before the NFL trade deadline, giving Patrick Mahomes a new target in exchange for two draft picks after weeks of searching for help at the position.

The deal

  • The terms. The Chiefs receive Downs and a 2027 seventh round pick, while the Colts receive 2027 fourth and fifth round picks.
  • The timing. The teams agreed to the trade just before the 4 p.m. ET deadline on Tuesday, in a buzzer beater deal reported by the Ringer's Tom Pelissero.
  • The wish. Downs requested a trade out of Indianapolis at the end of October, according to ESPN's Jeremy Fowler, after the Colts began selling off pieces of their roster, and the Chiefs deal grants that request.
  • The fit. Downs, primarily a slot receiver, is expected to slot in as Kansas City's No. 2 wideout behind Rashee Rice, filling a depth chart dented by injuries all season.
  • The price. The Colts had wanted a third round pick for Downs and had already received an offer of a fourth round pick, so Kansas City paid a fourth and a fifth to close the deal.

The player

  • The season. Downs, 25, has caught 33 passes for 354 yards and one touchdown in nine games this season, in his fourth season since the Colts took him in the third round of the 2023 draft.
  • The career. He has 2,494 career receiving yards and 12 touchdowns, and he has built a reputation as one of the league's most reliable separators at the position.
  • The contract. Downs is in the final year of his four year, $5.52 million rookie deal and becomes a free agent in March unless Kansas City offers him an extension, so the Chiefs are renting him for the rest of the season.
  • The upgrade. Downs moves from a 2-7 Colts team to a Chiefs offense led by Mahomes, a far better stage to play for a second contract.

The Colts fire sale

  • The fourth sale. Downs is the fourth Colts player traded this season, after guard Quenton Nelson, defensive tackle DeForest Buckner and receiver Keenan Allen, who went to the 49ers earlier on Tuesday.
  • The haul. Indianapolis has received five picks in the 2027 draft plus an additional pick in 2028 in return for the four players, stockpiling capital for whoever is hired as the next general manager.
  • The trigger. The sell off began after the Colts fired general manager Chris Ballard in October, following a 26-20 home loss to the Tennessee Titans, and the team has now lost five straight games.
  • The keepers. The Colts opted to keep running back Jonathan Taylor and quarterback Daniel Jones on the roster, even after weeks of trade speculation around both.

What comes next

  • The debut. Downs joins a Chiefs team in the middle of a playoff race, and his first game in red will show how quickly Mahomes trusts a receiver who arrived midseason.
  • The Steelers. Pittsburgh had also targeted Downs and offered a Day 3 pick, but the Chiefs' extra fifth round pick won the bidding, leaving the Steelers without a move at the deadline.
  • The Colts. Indianapolis is left with a wide receiver room of Laquon Treadwell and Ashton Dulin, and the rest of the season is now about the draft picks the fire sale produced.

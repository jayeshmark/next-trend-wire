About

Next Trend Wire is an independent digital news publication. It covers trending topics in the United States and around the world, plus startup and business news, and publishes around the clock as stories develop.

The publication is written and published by Jayesh Marathe. Every article is built from primary documents, not from other news coverage, and a story is published only when the news is new.

Next Trend Wire is independent. It is not affiliated with any news outlet, political party, or company it covers.

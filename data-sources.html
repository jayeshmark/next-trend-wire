Data sources

This page names the sources behind the numbers in the NFL database on Next Trend Wire.

All statistics, scores, schedules, rosters, and snap counts in the NFL database come from nflverse, published as nflverse-data on GitHub. The data is licensed under CC BY 4.0. Next Trend Wire presents the data in its own layout and words, with its own totals and tables.

The database covers seasons from 1999 to the present. Data for the 2026 season arrives as nflverse publishes it, and the database is refreshed on an ongoing basis.

Player photos on player pages are freely licensed images from Wikimedia Commons. Each photo is captioned with its source file and license.

Team and player names are used factually to report statistics. Next Trend Wire is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NFL, any team, or nflverse.
